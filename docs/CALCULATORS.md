# Calculator reference

MechCopilot ships 27 calculators. Each one shows an annotated diagram, live inputs with unit switching, pass/warn/fail checks, plots and the full worked solution. Open them from the **Calculator Library** or let the copilot choose them for you.

_This file is generated by `backend/scripts/gen_docs.py`._

## Fasteners

### Bolt group under eccentric shear
`bolt_group_eccentric` — Forces in each bolt of a rectangular or circular pattern loaded off-centre (bracket, console), critical bolt and utilisation.

- **Inputs:** Bolt pattern, Rows, Columns, Column pitch (x), Row pitch (y), Bolts on circle, Pitch-circle radius, Vertical load F, Eccentricity e (from centroid), Thread, Property class, Thread in the shear plane
- **Key results:** Critical bolt force, Utilisation, Critical bolt
- **References:** Shigley's Mechanical Engineering Design, 10th ed., ch. 8 §8-12 (shear joints); EN 1993-1-8:2005 Design of joints (Eurocode 3); ISO 898-1:2013 Mechanical properties of fasteners (steel)

### Bolt, nut & hole dimensions
`fastener_lookup` — Full ISO dimensions for a metric bolt: thread, head, nut, washer, clearance hole, tap drill, proof load and a recommended tightening torque.

- **Inputs:** Thread, Head type, Property class
- **Key results:** Stress area As, Assembly preload FM, Tightening torque MA
- **References:** ISO 261 / ISO 724 ISO general-purpose metric screw threads; ISO 898-1:2013 Mechanical properties of fasteners (steel); ISO 4762, ISO 4017, ISO 10642, ISO 4032, ISO 7089, ISO 273; VDI 2230-1:2015 Systematic calculation of highly stressed bolted joints

### Bolted joint under axial load (VDI 2230)
`bolted_joint` — Bolt and plate stiffness, load factor, embedding, residual clamp force, static, fatigue and surface-pressure checks with the joint diagram.

- **Inputs:** Thread, Property class, Head type, Joint type, Clamp length lK, Unthreaded shank inside clamp, Outer diameter of clamped parts DA, Clamped part material, Inner interfaces (plates − 1), Hardened washer under head (ISO 7089 200 HV), Axial load per bolt FA,max, Axial load per bolt FA,min, Required residual clamp FK,req, Lubrication / surface, Thread friction μG, Head friction μK, Tightening method
- **Key results:** Residual clamp force FKR, Load factor Φn, Static safety (yield), Fatigue safety
- **References:** VDI 2230-1:2015 Systematic calculation of highly stressed bolted joints; ISO 898-1:2013 Mechanical properties of fasteners (steel); Shigley's Mechanical Engineering Design, 10th ed., ch. 8

### Bolted lap joint in shear (EN 1993-1-8)
`bolt_shear_joint` — Bearing-type or slip-resistant (friction-grip) shear joint: bolt shear, plate bearing and slip resistance with edge-distance checks.

- **Inputs:** Thread, Property class, Number of bolts, Shear planes per bolt, Thread in the shear plane, Thinnest plate thickness t, Plate steel, End distance e1 (load direction), Edge distance e2 (⟂ load), Design shear load FEd (total), Joint category, Faying surface class, Hole type
- **Key results:** Joint resistance FRd, Utilisation FEd/FRd, Governing mode
- **References:** EN 1993-1-8:2005 Design of joints (Eurocode 3); EN 1090-2 Execution of steel structures (hole sizes); ISO 898-1:2013 Mechanical properties of fasteners (steel)

### Minimum thread engagement length
`thread_engagement` — Required depth of thread engagement so a tapped hole (steel, aluminium, cast iron, brass) does not strip before the bolt breaks.

- **Inputs:** Thread, Property class, Tapped part material, Actual engagement length LE
- **Key results:** Minimum engagement (theoretical), Required L / d, Strip force / bolt break force
- **References:** Machinery's Handbook, 31st ed. — thread stripping areas; VDI 2230-1:2015 Systematic calculation of highly stressed bolted joints; ISO 261 / ISO 724 ISO general-purpose metric screw threads

### Tightening torque & preload (VDI 2230)
`bolt_preload_torque` — Tightening torque for a target preload, with friction condition, tightening-method scatter and a friction sensitivity plot.

- **Inputs:** Thread, Property class, Head type, Clearance hole (ISO 273), Lubrication / surface, Thread friction μG, Head friction μK, Tightening method
- **Key results:** Tightening torque MA, Max assembly preload FM,max, Min assembly preload FM,min
- **References:** VDI 2230-1:2015 Systematic calculation of highly stressed bolted joints; ISO 898-1:2013 Mechanical properties of fasteners (steel); Shigley's Mechanical Engineering Design, 10th ed., ch. 8

### Which bolt? Fastener selector
`fastener_selector` — Smallest standard ISO bolt size and property class for an axial and/or transverse load, with stock length, torque and full designation.

- **Inputs:** Axial service load per bolt FA, Transverse load per bolt FQ, Interface friction μT, Safety factor on loads, Property class, Head type, Joint type, Clamp length, Lubrication / surface, Thread friction μG, Head friction μK, Tightening method
- **Key results:** Recommended fastener, Tightening torque, Min preload FM,min
- **References:** VDI 2230-1:2015 Systematic calculation of highly stressed bolted joints; ISO 898-1:2013 Mechanical properties of fasteners (steel); ISO 4762, ISO 4017, ISO 4032, ISO 7089; Shigley's Mechanical Engineering Design, 10th ed., ch. 8
